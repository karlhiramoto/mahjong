🀄 How to Play Mahjong

🎯 Objective

Clear all 144 tiles from the board by finding and matching identical pairs of Free tiles.

🔓 When is a Tile "Free"?

You can only pick a tile if it satisfies both conditions:

  • No tile on top: No other tile is covering or overlapping it from a higher layer.
  • Open side: At least one horizontal side (Left or Right) is completely clear with no adjacent tile on the same layer.
💡 Tip: Blocked tiles are slightly dimmed and cannot be tapped until you uncover or unblock them.

🀄 Matching Rules

Numbered Suits (Exact Match)

Dots ⚪, Bamboo 🎋, Characters 萬 (1–9). Must match the exact same number and suit (e.g. 5-Bamboo matches 5-Bamboo).

Honors (Exact Match)

Winds (東, 南, 西, 北) and Dragons (中 Red, 發 Green, 🔲 White). Match the identical symbol.

🌸 Seasons (Match Any)

Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter. Any Season matches any other Season!

🌺 Flowers (Match Any)

Plum, Orchid, Chrysanthemum, Bamboo. Any Flower matches any other Flower!

🛠️ Game Features & Toolbar

  • 💡 Hint (H): Highlights a recommended matching open pair and records your hints used.
  • ↩ Undo (U): Reverts your previous move.
  • 🔀 Shuffle (S): Rearranges remaining active tiles if no moves are left.
  • 📐 3D View (V): Toggles between the 3D angled elevation view and classic 2D top-down view.
  • 🔍 Fit (F): Centers and zooms the board to fit your display.
  • 🤖 Auto-Play: Watch the autonomous AI solver play through the game.

📱 Touch & Desktop Controls

  • Tap / Click: Select a free tile, then tap its match to clear both.
  • Pinch to Zoom: Use two fingers on mobile (or mouse wheel on desktop) to zoom in and out.
  • Drag to Pan: Touch and drag (or click & drag) to move across the board when zoomed in.
